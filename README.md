# 📊 Myntra Online Retail Sales Analysis (Excel + SQL + Power BI)

A complete **end‑to‑end Data Analyst portfolio project** built using real‑world online retail transaction data. This project focuses on **business KPIs, customer behavior, product performance, and geographic insights**, and presents findings through an interactive **3‑page Power BI dashboard**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The dataset represents transactional sales data from an online retail company selling gift items globally. The objective of this project is to help stakeholders:

* Understand revenue trends
* Identify high‑value customers
* Optimize product and inventory strategy
* Reduce cancellations and operational losses

The analysis was performed using **SQL for data cleaning & KPI calculations** and **Power BI for data visualization and storytelling**.

---

## 🧠 Business Problems Addressed

* 📉 Why are some months underperforming in revenue?
* 🔁 Are customers returning after their first purchase?
* ❌ Which products contribute most to cancellations?
* 🏆 Which products and customers drive maximum revenue?
* 🌍 Which countries are the strongest and weakest markets?

## 📈 Key Insights

* A small percentage of customers contribute a large share of total revenue (Pareto principle).
* Certain products have high sales volume but low revenue contribution.
* Cancellations are concentrated around specific products, indicating quality or logistics issues.
* The UK dominates revenue, but non‑UK markets show strong growth potential.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

* Introduce loyalty programs for high‑value repeat customers.
* Improve quality checks for products with high cancellation rates.
* Optimize inventory planning based on top‑selling products.
* Expand marketing efforts in high‑potential non‑UK countries.
